What Second Opinion is — and isn’t

Second Opinion gives you fast, repeatable snapshots of your current cognitive performance and selected operational risks. It’s an extra data point for your IM SAFE and Aeronautical Decision Making (ADM) flow.

It does not replace the regulations, your POH, your CFI, your own judgment, or common sense. You are always the pilot in command.

Using Second Opinion effectively

Second Opinion is meant to be quick. A full Cognition & Acuity run typically takes a few minutes; a FRAT can often be completed in under two.

  • Run the Cognition & Acuity test at various times (rested, tired, stressed) to build a baseline.
  • Use FRAT on days when the operation, environment, or passengers add complexity.
  • Look for trends across days, not just a single “good” or “bad” score.

The app can’t make a go/no-go decision for you, but it can nudge you toward more deliberate choices.

About Second Opinion

Second Opinion combines three classic cognitive paradigms — simple reaction time, Stroop, and 2-back — into a compact, pilot-oriented self-check.

The scoring emphasizes stability and trends more than any single trial. A “bad” result is a cue to pause and reassess, not a diagnosis or a grounding order.

Second Opinion is a supplemental decision aid. All responsibility for flight decisions rests with the pilot in command.

About On Second Thought

On Second Thought is a show that dives deep into single aviation accidents — not to assign blame, but to understand the chain of decisions, pressures, and human factors that led there.

Second Opinion grew out of the same mission: help pilots notice subtle trends earlier, when they’re still easy to correct.

A short, repeatable series of tasks tuned for pilot cognition and acuity.

What this assessment measures

  • Simple reaction time – how quickly you respond to a visual change.
  • Stroop interference – executive control and resistance to distraction.
  • 2-back – short-term working memory and sustained attention.
