About Mental Status

Mental Status is an experimental, on-device tool inspired by IM SAFE and human factors research. It samples three domains:

  • Reaction: how quickly you respond to a simple “go” cue.
  • Color–Word (Stroop): how well you resist interference between words and colors.
  • 2-back: a small working-memory task, similar to what’s used in many lab studies.

The goal isn’t to diagnose anything. It’s to give you a private, repeatable sense of whether today feels like one of your “good” days, a “soft” day, or a “not today” day—before you add the workload of a flight.

All data stays on this device. It’s a supplement to IM SAFE and good ADM, not a replacement.

Risk bands (reference)

After scoring, your total will be placed into one of these reference bands. High-band scores are a cue to slow down and think hard about mitigation, alternatives, or delay.
IFR
<100 in type: Low 20–25, Moderate 25–30, High >30
≥100 in type: Low 25–30, Moderate 30–35, High >35
VFR
<100 in type: Low 5–15, Moderate 15–20, High >20
≥100 in type: Low 15–20, Moderate 20–25, High >25
All items are True/False. “True” applies the score shown in the FAA table for that condition; “False” contributes zero.
