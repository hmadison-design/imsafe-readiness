IM SAFE — Two-Minute Drill

A ~2-minute, baseline-referenced snapshot of cognitive speed, attention control, and working memory. Local & private v0.6 experimental

How it works

  • Reaction (30s) • Color-Word (45s) • 2-back (5 groups)
  • Compare to your baseline (T-scores for Stroop)
  • Qualitative status: Near / Below / Well below baseline
